# Incident 2026-08-02 — inventory noise, no checkout rollback

A noisy `inventory-api` stock sync and an unrelated batch job missed SLA. checkout-api error_rate stayed near 3% with 200s on `/health`.

On-call almost rolled back checkout-api v2.8.0. The decision agent recommended **INVESTIGATE**. Inventory retries cleared the backlog; checkout was left in place.

Lesson: unrelated errors are not a checkout rollback.
